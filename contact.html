Questions?
We're listening.

For listing reports, rights concerns, account help, privacy requests, or security issues, email rights@rustports.com.

Report a listing or rights concern

Include the project name, its GitHub URL or RustPorts listing ID, the specific concern, and a way to contact you. If you represent a rights holder, identify the relevant work and your authority to act. Link to supporting information rather than sending game files. Do not include passwords, tokens, or unrelated personal information.

Moderators review reports and may hide a listing while investigating, request clarification from the creator or reporter, or remove a listing. A listing can be reported even after approval. Approval is not a legal or security guarantee. Please report honestly and do not use the process to harass other creators.

Manage your own listing

Sign in with GitHub and open My dashboard. Edit a listing to correct its details or address. Changes return it to private review. Withdraw a listing to hide it from the catalog. Contact moderation to appeal a decision or request restoration of a withdrawn listing.

Account and privacy requests

For deletion, correction, or access requests, include your GitHub username and the action you want. We will verify ownership before acting. Read the privacy notice for retention details.

Security concerns

Send a short description, affected URL, and non-destructive reproduction steps. Do not access another person's account, extract private records, upload exploit files, or disrupt servers. Creator-operated game servers are outside RustPorts' control.

Not an emergency service

We do not promise immediate responses or round-the-clock monitoring. For urgent danger, contact the appropriate local authority. This contact process is not a claim of registered-agent status or a guarantee of any statutory safe harbor.
